    Named but not located in

    Names the extractor found that WHG’s index could not match inside this county. Matching is strict — a name must match a record exactly — so this list holds three kinds of thing: real places the gazetteer does not yet know, names the sources spell differently from the index, and people, which is what searching inside a county is for. Loosening the match to catch the first two reliably placed the third, so it was not kept.

      This ran unattended. Every place here was found in the prose, matched and located without anyone intervening — that is the point. It is not a black box, though: the Workbench stops for a person wherever you want it to. Its review stage offers the rival candidates for an ambiguous name, takes a rejection, lets you search by hand for one the index missed and record why. Nothing here needed that; anyone publishing from it would use it — and the not-located list above is where they would start.
